## Uses the author considers commercial

These uses are primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation, and need separate written permission:

- **Paid services** — offering RuleNova rule sets, or services built on them, as part of a paid or freemium product
- **API resale** — exposing RuleNova data through a commercial API
- **OEM / white-label** — bundling RuleNova into a product sold to third parties
- **Paid redistribution** — redistributing RuleNova data or outputs as part of a paid product or subscription
- **Business deployment** — rolling RuleNova out across an organization's network or devices as part of its business operations

## Uses that do not need permission

- Personal use, including an individual's own devices at home or at work
- Academic research and education
- Non-commercial open-source projects that keep the attribution and use CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for adapted rule sets
- Evaluation and testing

## Getting permission

RuleNova's rule sets are generated from [IPNova](https://github.com/harryheros/ipnova) and [DomainNova](https://github.com/harryheros/domainnova). Commercial permission is arranged together with those datasets: see the commercial licensing page of [IPNova](https://github.com/harryheros/ipnova/blob/main/COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md) or [DomainNova](https://github.com/harryheros/domainnova/blob/main/COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

Please describe your intended use: which rule sets, what they are for, and roughly how widely they will be used.
